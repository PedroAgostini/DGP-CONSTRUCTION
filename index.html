Our core services

Trim

The difference between average carpentry and high-end work comes down to fractions of an inch. We measure twice, cut once, and make sure your woodwork fits flush against your walls.

Window installation

A badly installed window leaks air, drives up your heating bills, and lets water rot your internal framing. We install and replace windows the right way.

Deck building

When the weather turns nice in New England, your deck is where you spend your weekends. We design and build brand-new custom outdoor decks engineered for structural strength and long-term durability.

Deck flooring

If your existing deck frame is still structurally sound but the boards are rotting, splintering, or faded, you don't need a full rebuild, you need new deck flooring.

Cabinet installation

Installing cabinets sounds simple until you realize your floors aren't level and your drywall bows out. We shim, level, and flush-mount cabinet systems so every door swings straight and every drawer slides smoothly.

General remodeling

Looking to open up a room, finish a basement, or update your main living space? We handle complete interior remodeling projects, bringing our precision finish-carpentry background to every phase of the build.
