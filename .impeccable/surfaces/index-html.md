# Surface brief: index.html (one page site)

Scope: single page institutional site, Persuade mode. Audience: CT/NY homeowners 35 to 65 arriving from ads, mostly mobile. Action: request a 24-hour itemized quote (form, text, email). Proof on hand: logo, client-declared licensed & insured, 24h quote promise. No real photos or reviews yet: stock images and example testimonials marked for replacement.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every section is a millwork shop drawing sheet. Precision is proven by dimension strings in fractions of an inch, details and schedules, before the copy claims it. Refuses the category default of a dark photo hero with a badge strip and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: White vellum sheets ruled in black linework, logo gold as the redline and revision cloud, black sheets for the offer and notes. Archivo Expanded heavy for headings echoing the DGP letterforms, Archivo for text, Martian Mono only for measurements and sheet data. Title blocks, finish schedule table, numbered general notes, detail bubbles, a site plan for the service radius.

STORY: The visitor sees their house drawn with care, understands DGP sweats fractions of an inch, believes it through Detail A versus Detail B and the schedule, then sends photos by text or form for a quote in 24 hours.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 6 columns: H1 in expanded heavy sentence case (cited deviation from caps: the 50-character H1 in expanded caps runs to 6+ lines at 390 and pushes the primary button below the fold; sentence case keeps 4 lines), sub, gold quote button plus Text Us button, title block reading QUOTE DUE 24 HRS spanning full width directly under the split, inside the 1440x900 fold. Right 6 columns (the expanded face needs the width): window and casing elevation drafting itself with dimension strings and a gold revision cloud on the miter. Header with logo, anchors, phone.

FORM: The Shop Drawing, position 1 on my ordered list, seed key 8b2f8e3d (chosen as Impeccable's pick).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: drawings draft themselves line by line (stroke reveal), then dimension strings snap in; same drafting grammar for hero, details and site plan. Content visible by default; reduced motion skips it.
