# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Deploy target: Hostinger (migrating off Wix). Form is frontend only; lead delivery integration is handled by the team later.

## Users

US homeowners aged 35 to 65 within a 30 mile radius of Danbury, CT (Fairfield County, Northern Westchester County). They own the house, notice bad trim, and want a contractor who shows up, quotes fast, and leaves the house clean. They prefer text message and email over phone calls. Secondary (hypothesis, unconfirmed): builders and general contractors who need a reliable finish carpentry sub.

## Product Purpose

One page institutional site for DGP Construction LLC that turns paid traffic (Google Ads, Meta Ads, GBP) into quote requests. Success is more direct homeowner jobs (target +2 to +4 jobs) and less reliance on subcontracting and Thumbtack.

## Positioning

Finish carpentry specialist, not a generalist remodeler. The promise is precision at fractions of an inch (tight scarf joints, plumb cabinets, square casings) plus an itemized quote within 24 hours.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive mostly on mobile from ads. They send photos, measurements or blueprints to get a quote. Contact channels: phone/SMS (914) 538-0739, email (placeholder info@dgpconstruction.com until the domain exists), Instagram @dgpconstruction_.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services shown: Trim / finish carpentry, Window Installation, Deck Building, Deck Flooring, Cabinet Installation, General Remodeling. Siding is offered but intentionally left off the site.
- Service area: 30 mile radius around Danbury, CT. Do not publish the street address; show service area only.
- Company founded 2025; owner has 4+ years of carpentry experience; team of three.
- Do not claim architect/designer support until validated.

## Brand Commitments

- Name DGP Construction and the DGP initials must appear. Logo files in LOGOS/ (color, black, white).
- Client colors: black and gold, white approved as complement.
- Voice: formal, objective, direct, American English. No em dashes, no AI filler phrasing.
- Copy source of truth: "ONE PAGE - DGP CONSTRUCTION.md".

## Evidence on Hand

- Client supplied a Google Drive folder with 134 project images. All received a visual content triage; 21 finalists were inspected at full size. Seven selected photographs, cropped to clean installed work, are active as WebP. See PHOTO_SELECTION.md and docs/images/SELECTION.json. Legacy stock files were removed from assets/img. At the client’s subsequent request, two generated illustrative images complement the Window Installation card and Why Homeowners Call Us First background. See docs/images/GENERATED.json; these do not represent DGP project photographs.
- No real reviews yet. Example testimonials are allowed in the layout only when clearly marked in code as placeholders to replace before launch.
- No badges, certifications or payment methods confirmed. Licensed and insured is declared by the client.

## Product Principles

1. Every section moves the visitor toward a quote; the 24 hour quote is the core offer.
2. Show craft through detail, never through superlatives.
3. Never fabricate proof: reviews, badges and numbers must be real before launch.
4. Text and email first, phone second, matching how the audience prefers to talk.
