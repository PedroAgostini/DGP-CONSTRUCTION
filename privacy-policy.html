Who we are

DGP Construction LLC (“DGP Construction,” “we,” “us”) is a finish carpentry and home improvement contractor based in Danbury, Connecticut. This policy applies to this website and to the conversations that follow a quote request made through it.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote, we ask for the service you need, your town or ZIP code, project details, your name, your mobile phone number and your email address. When you text, call or email us, we receive whatever you choose to share, such as photos, measurements or blueprints.

Information collected automatically. This website does not use analytics tools, advertising pixels or cookies of its own. Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time, to keep the site secure and running.

Embedded map. The service area map is provided by Google Maps. When the map loads, Google may collect information such as your IP address and may set its own cookies, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How we use your information

  • To prepare and send your itemized quote.
  • To contact you by text, phone or email about your request and your project.
  • To schedule and carry out work you hire us to do.
  • To keep records of quotes and jobs and to meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • To protect this website against misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number, you agree that we may text you about your quote and your project. These are conversation messages about your request, not marketing. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. We do not share your mobile number or your consent to receive texts with third parties for their own marketing.

How we share information

We share personal information only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as website hosting, email and messaging services, and the tool we use to receive quote requests. They may use it only to provide those services to us.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public.
  • As part of a sale, merger or transfer of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to answer your request and complete any work, and afterward for as long as needed for our records, warranty questions and legal or tax obligations. If a quote request does not lead to a project, we delete the information once we no longer need it.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You can ask us to stop emailing you.

Depending on where you live, including Connecticut, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond as the law requires and will not treat you differently for making a request.

Security

This website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is meant for homeowners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other sites

This website links to services we do not control, such as Instagram and Google Maps. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page.
